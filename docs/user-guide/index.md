# Garment ERP User Guide

This guide is for the people who run a Thai garment or custom-apparel factory on Garment ERP every day: the owner or general manager, the accountant, sales and warehouse staff, production leads and floor operators, the HR officer, and the factory's own system administrator. It explains, in plain business terms, how to do your daily work in the system: quoting and invoicing customers, receiving and issuing stock, tracking work orders on the floor, paying staff, reading reports, and managing who can do what.

## How to use this guide

- Start with [Getting started](00-getting-started.md). It covers signing in, finding your way around the menus, switching between Thai and English, and the conventions used on every screen.
- Then go to the chapters for your job. The table at the bottom of this page tells you which ones.
- Each chapter is split into tasks. Each task has numbered steps, one action per step. Button and menu names are shown in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen. The first time a menu appears in a chapter, its Thai label follows in brackets.
- Boxes marked **Note**, **Tip** and **Warning** point out things that are easy to miss. A Warning means you could lose work or record something wrong.
- You only see the menus and buttons your role allows. If a screen in this guide is missing for you, ask your system administrator. They can give you access as described in [Admin & Access](06-admin-access.md).

> **Note:** Where the product does not yet do something you might expect, the guide says so plainly and tells you what to do instead. These notes are labelled as current limitations.
